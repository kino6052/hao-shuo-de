Hao-shuo-de has {{dictionaryCount}} words. For everything else, you put them together: a book is {{word:xie3}}-{{word:de}} {{word:dong1xi}} ("a written thing"), and a teacher is {{word:bang1}}-{{word:ren2}}-{{word:xue2}}-{{word:de}} {{word:ren2}} ("the person who helps people learn").

This list covers the 1,000 most common Chinese words and shows what Hao-shuo-de says for each one, in alphabetical order. Each entry says how well it works: **word** (Hao-shuo-de has it), **natural** (Mandarin says it this way too), **plain** (correct and understood, but not the usual way), **gap** (no good way yet), **skip** (not needed), or **name** (in quotes). The list grows in phases.

Hao-shuo-de has {{dictionaryCount}} words. For everything else, you put them together: a book is {{word:xie3}}-{{word:de}} {{word:dong1xi}} ("a written thing"), and to buy is {{word:gei3}}-{{word:jin1}}-{{word:de2}}-{{word:dong1xi}} ("give money, get things").

This list starts with the 500 most common Chinese words and shows what Hao-shuo-de says for each one. Each entry says how well it works: **word** (Hao-shuo-de has it), **natural** (Mandarin says it this way too), **plain** (correct and understood, but not the usual way), **gap** (no good way yet), **skip** (not needed), or **name** (in quotes). The list grows in phases.

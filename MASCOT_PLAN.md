# Miao-miao: mascot and story plan

A cat, a student and a year in one neighborhood, to run through the whole book. The idea: the world is complicated, but thinking and speaking do not have to be. Miao-miao shows this by what it does, not by explaining it.

Status: **decided, 2026-10-15** (M1–M20, asked one at a time). M1 to M26 are decided. §5 to §10 (grounded image mapping, intro images, the new story intro, app needs, work order) are the plan the author's answers in §11 settled; the work has not started. Three images exist so far (chapters 1 to 3 of the first draft; only chapter 3 fits its lesson).

## 2. First image: the character sheet (Doubao)

One sheet with all five characters, so one style is fixed before any chapter. Prompt, English:

> A character sheet on a plain white background, minimalist black line art, thick even outlines, no shading, no fill color. Five characters standing side by side with space between them. 1) A round white cat like a soft bean, two small triangle ears, two dot eyes, a tiny curved smile, a small tail curled up. 2) A simple human student figure with a round head, dot eyes, a tiny smile, simple short hair drawn in a few clean lines, a plain coat and a backpack, no gender signs. 3) A small girl, a round head with two small buns, dot eyes, a tiny smile, a plain dress, clearly smaller than the student. 4) A very small round mouse with two round ears, dot eyes and a thin tail. 5) A round small bird with a dot eye, a tiny beak and two stick legs. Cute, calm, very simple, the same line weight for all five. No text.

Prompt, Chinese (use if the English comes out too detailed):

> 白色背景上的角色设定图，极简黑色线条画，线条粗细一致，没有阴影，没有填充颜色。五个角色并排站立，中间留有空白。1）一只像软豆子一样的圆圆的白猫，两只小三角耳朵，两个圆点眼睛，一个小小的微笑，小尾巴向上卷起。2）一个简单的学生，圆脑袋，点状眼睛，小小的微笑，画有简单的短发（用几笔干净的线条勾出），穿普通外套，背着书包，没有性别特征。3）一个小女孩，圆脑袋，头顶两个小发髻，点状眼睛，小小的微笑，穿普通裙子，比学生矮很多。4）一只很小的圆形老鼠，两只圆耳朵，点状眼睛，细细的尾巴。5）一只圆圆的小鸟，点状眼睛，小小的嘴，两条细腿。可爱、安静、非常简单，五个角色线条粗细相同。不要文字。

How to use the result: keep the best sheet, check it against §1 (does Miao-miao look like the reference picture?), then reuse its cast text in every chapter prompt. If a character drifts, repeat the §1 line for that character and nothing else.

## 5. Why a new plan, and the rules for it (proposed, 2026-10-15)

The first three images were written from lesson titles and story beats. Checked against the lessons, only chapter 3 fits (lesson 3 teaches "a big place", "many people"). Chapter 1 shows an arrival while lesson 1 teaches pinyin and tones; chapter 2 shows "two words, a whole thought" while lesson 2 teaches "X is Y" and "X is not Y". So the picture of a lesson must come from the lesson, and the story rides on top.

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| P1 | **One taught sentence per image.** Pick it from the lesson's "you'll be able to say" list or its modules' examples. The scene acts that sentence out literally; the story context (season, who is there, the owner's notes) is secondary. |
| P2 | **The caption is that sentence.** Miao-miao's line in Hao-shuo-de first, the Chinese beneath, then the plain-English meaning in the lesson's language. Words only from this lesson and earlier ones, checked by the book's early-words and parse gates. |
| P3 | **No text drawn inside images.** The generator garbles letters and hanzi. Allowed symbols: question mark, equals and not-equals, digits, tone lines, and only where the lesson teaches them. Any readable text (the finale note) is real page text laid over the picture, never part of the image. |
| P4 | **Bubbles hold pictures, not words** (chapters 2 and 3 show this works). |
| P5 | **Files are named by lesson id**, not number (the book moves lessons by editing `book.js` only): `public/story/<lesson-id>.webp`. The season and accent color are data next to the id, so a moved lesson keeps its picture. |
| P6 | **Alt text in en, ru and zh for every image**, describing the sentence being acted out. |
| P7 | **One accent color per season** (M16), with one planned exception: the colors lesson (several colors on purpose). The four-seasons strip in the story intro has one panel per season, each with its own single color (M26). |
| P8 | **Small casts.** Two or three named characters per image; the whole cast only in lesson 14 (we all eat), where togetherness is the point. |
| P9 | **A check before an image is accepted:** (1) does it show the sentence from P1 without a caption? (2) the cat, student, girl, mouse and bird look like the sheet; (3) no text, no hatching or texture; (4) the accent color is used on one or two things only; (5) nothing in it contradicts a lesson (nothing the lesson has not taught is needed to read it). |

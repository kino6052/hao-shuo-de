## How the numbers are made

- **Cost** of dropping a word: composites left with no form (weighted by rank: top 1000 count 3, top 2000 count 2, the rest 1), composites losing a form, composites hanging under it, lesson lines to rewrite, composite examples, other mentions, coverage lost or touched, other words that name it, catch-all senses left empty, opposite and close pairs that break, and the Mandarin words that could no longer be written.
- **Weights** (`COSTS` in `src/lib/kb/candidates.js`): redo 1, loses form 0.25, head 0.5, lessons 1, examples 0.25, mentions 0.5, coverage lost 10, coverage touched 1, definitions 1, senses 2, relations 2, Mandarin 1. These are my first guesses; change them and run the audit again.
- **Mandarin** is the first 6000 words of the frequency list. A word can be written when each of its hanzi is a Hao-shuo-de word (or a sense of one), or the word itself is in the dictionary.
- The counts are checked against the solver's removal report (`kb remove`), which also gives the reason for every line.

# Jack and the Beanstalk

- **English:** Jack and his mother had no money, only one cow. (Literally: "an animal that says 'moo'.") His mother said: "Take the cow, sell it, and bring the money back." On the road, an old man he'd never seen said: "Give me your cow, and I'll give you five magic beans." (Literally: "five little round things never seen before.") Jack went home. His mother was furious and threw the beans out of the window. That night, a plant came up out of the beans. It grew and grew, far up into the sky. Jack climbed up. At the top there was a huge house. In the house lived a giant (literally: "a really big person"). He had lots of gold. While the giant slept, Jack took his gold and hurried down. The giant woke up, saw Jack, and came down the plant after him! Jack broke the plant with a tool (an axe). The plant and the giant came crashing down, and the giant died. Jack and his mother had plenty of money and lived happily (literally: "lived very well").

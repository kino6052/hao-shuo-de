# The Tortoise and the Hare

- **English:** There was a hare with very fast feet, and a tortoise with a hard shell (literally: "hard on the outside"), who was very slow. The hare laughed: "You're so slow!" The tortoise said: "Let's race, and see who gets to that big tree first!" They set off. The hare was soon far ahead. He looked back: the tortoise was far behind. He said: "I'll take a little nap, it doesn't matter." He lay down by the road and fell asleep. The tortoise was very slow, but she didn't stop and didn't sleep; she kept going, little by little. She reached the sleeping hare, and then passed him. The hare woke up and looked: the tortoise was almost at the big tree! He ran with all his strength, but the tortoise got there ahead of him. Being slow doesn't matter. If you don't stop, you will get there.

# The Fisherman and the Golden Fish

- **English:** An old man and his old wife lived by the sea (literally: "a very big water"). They were poor (literally: "had no money"). The old man fished (literally: "hunted water animals"). One day he caught a golden fish (literally: "a yellow water animal"). The golden fish spoke: "Put me back in the water! Whatever you want, I'll give you." The old man was kind. He asked for nothing and put it back in the water. He went home and told his wife. She was furious: "You fool! Our trough is broken! Go and ask for a good one!" The golden fish gave them a good trough. But the old woman said: "I want a big house!" They got a big house. Then the old woman said: "I want lots of money!" They got lots of money. Again she said: "I want to be queen!" (Literally: "the biggest person.") At last she said: "I want the whole sea to be mine, and the golden fish must obey me!" The golden fish said nothing. It just swam off into the water and never came back. The old man went home: the big house was gone, the money was gone. The old woman sat beside the broken trough.

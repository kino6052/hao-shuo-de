# Miao-miao stories: preview of the reading texts, lessons 2 to 26

Status: a preview for you to review, written 2026-10-19. Nothing in the lesson modules has changed.
It follows WRITING_PLAN.md (steps 1 to 5, rules W1 to W18) and tells the Miao-miao story of MASCOT_PLAN.md (cast M13 to M25, one picture sentence per lesson P1).

## What this is

For every lesson from 2 to 26, one short text about the cat Miao-miao and the student who arrives for one school year. It starts with two sentences in lesson 2 and grows a little in every lesson, because it may use only the words and structures of the lessons before and including its own.

For each lesson the preview shows five things, in the order of the writing plan:

1. **The English text**, written first and as the story wants it (W12).
2. **The same story re-expressed in simplest ideas**: short Hao-shuo-de sentences, each one parsed with only what that lesson has taught, with its plain English reading beside it.
3. **The structures** it needs, each with the lesson that teaches it. A structure from a later lesson never appears (this is checked by the parser, not by eye).
4. **The words**: which of the lesson's new words the text uses, which it leaves out, and how many words it reaches back for.
5. **The gaps**: what the English says that the Hao-shuo-de cannot say yet, and what was done about it (W9: gaps are logged, not dodged).

The Hao-shuo-de lines are drafts for checking the plan. The final texts are written lesson by lesson in step 3b once you have approved the English (W12).

## The story in four colors

| Season | Lessons | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Autumn | 2 to 7 | The student meets Miao-miao, the bird, the girl and the mouse. Everyone is named or pointed at; the first note on the door asks who the student is. |
| Winter | 8 to 13 | Cold, sleep, a missing cat, a visitor from far away, hot water, bags that look the same. |
| Spring | 14 to 20 | Everyone eats together; the room is tidied and the toy mended; a lost bag; counting; one red thing; the bird mixes up words. |
| Summer | 21 to 26 | Giving and doing for someone; being late; hello and goodbye; the year ends; the moon; the student writes the answer to every note. |

The owner is never seen. Notes on the door ask one question each (from lesson 6 on); a cup of hot water appears on the step; in lesson 26 a hand brings hot water and Miao-miao takes the student's note.
